The voice

A narrator with the confidence of someone reading a cereal box at a funeral. Deadpan, a little too sincere, and impossible to tune out.

The one rule we never break: the joke never undercuts the safety. Every procedure, spec, and warning comes from your source material and gets checked before it ships. If we don't know something, we ask. We don't guess.

How it works

From your SOP to the screen.

  1. Source it

    You send the procedures, docs, and the people who know the job. That becomes our source of truth.

  2. Script it

    We write the script and flag every gap before a single frame is shot. You approve it.

  3. Shoot it

    Real footage on your floor, cleared so no customer data, paperwork, or tags show on screen.

  4. Ship it

    Captioned films plus matching SOPs and packets, ready for orientation day.
